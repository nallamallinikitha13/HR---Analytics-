# 📊 HR Analytics Dashboard Project

## 📌 Project Overview
This project focuses on analyzing employee attrition patterns using HR data and building interactive dashboards with Excel, SQL, Power BI, and Tableau.

The objective is to understand workforce trends across departments, job roles, monthly income, work-life balance, working years, and promotion history.

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies
- **SQL:** Data analysis and KPI queries
- **Microsoft Excel:** Pivot tables, charts, and interactive slicers
- **Power BI:** KPI cards and interactive reporting
- **Tableau:** Executive dashboards and visual storytelling

## 📈 Key Performance Indicators
1. Attrition Rate by Department
2. Average Hourly Rate of Male Research Scientists
3. Average Monthly Income by Attrition Status
4. Average Working Years by Department
5. Work-Life Balance by Job Role
6. Attrition Rate vs. Years Since Last Promotion

## 🔍 Key Insights
- Department-wise attrition rates fall within approximately 49%–51%.
- Research & Development shows a slightly higher attrition rate than other departments.
- Average monthly income is similar for employees who left and those who stayed.
- Work-life balance scores are relatively consistent across job roles.
- Attrition rates fluctuate at higher values of years since the last promotion.

## 💡 Recommendations
- Introduce regular career development and promotion discussions.
- Review retention challenges in Research & Development.
- Improve work-life balance through workload reviews and flexible work options.
- Consider recognition, career growth, and manager support alongside compensation.
